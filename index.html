About 03

Nicola Pardy is a writer, editorial strategist, and producer living in New York.

She's produced video work for outlets including Vogue, The Wall Street Journal Magazine, Refinery29, and Sony Music — covering artists, designers, athletes, and the culture that connects them.

She holds a master's degree in Gender & Social Policy from the London School of Economics, and has written on current events, gender, and visual culture for The New York Times, The Outline, and Refinery29.

Her newsletter, Night Toast, explores ideas about art, the internet, belonging, and place — close readings of the ordinary.
